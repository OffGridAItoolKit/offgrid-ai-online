Welcome to OffGrid AI Field Guide. This app uses online AI responses and practical phone tools for field-ready help.

Camera, microphone, media, and compass access are requested only when you tap those features.

Photos, videos, and prompts are sent to the online AI service only when you choose to use those features.

Chats are not saved to an OffGrid AI account or personal cloud history. Online providers process requests under their own privacy policies; no account is required for v1.

Health information: OffGrid AI Field Guide is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Photo and video analysis may provide general educational health or first-aid information, but it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

AI can make mistakes. For medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, consult a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services. Verify other important survival, legal, financial, or safety details before acting.
