The Process

How the Council Thinks

A four-stage pipeline executed server-side. From question to answer in under 40 seconds. Fully transparent.

01
No Collaboration. No Influence.

Independent Responses

Each model receives the question and generates a complete answer on its own. No shared context. No chain-of-thought passing. Four independent perspectives from four different architectures.

02
Blind Ranking. No Favoritism.

Anonymous Peer Review

Each model reviews all four answers anonymously. The answers are labeled A, B, C, D and shuffled differently for each reviewer. No model can recognize its own answer or play favorites.

03
Mathematical Scoring. Merit Wins.

Chairman Election

Rankings are aggregated using a weighted Borda count where Accuracy counts double. The highest-scoring answer is elected Chairman for this specific query. No permanent leaders. Every question is a fresh competition.

04
Decide First. Then Improve.

Command Synthesis

Scout (GPT-5.2) serves as permanent Editor-in-Chief. It takes the Chairman's winning answer as the foundation and incorporates genuine improvements from the other three advisors. The result is a decisive, polished, expert-level response.
