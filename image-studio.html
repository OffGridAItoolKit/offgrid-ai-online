One Question. A Complete Field Kit.

Image Studio doesn't work alone. Inside the Command Center, a single question triggers a pipeline that goes from research to visual reference to actionable supply list — automatically.

Watch the full pipeline in action — prompt to printed document in under 2 minutes. Recorded on the OffGrid AI ToolKit USB drive.

1
Ask the AI Council

Ask a Question, Get a Full Article

Type a question into the Command Center. The AI Council — multiple specialized models debating and synthesizing — produces a comprehensive, structured knowledge base article. Not a chatbot answer. A real reference document.

Example: "How do I purify water without a filter?" → The AI Council generates a full article covering source selection, clarification, boiling, chemical disinfection (chlorine dioxide, bleach, iodine), and solar disinfection — with safety notes for each method.
2
Generate a Visual

Turn That Article Into a Diagram

Save the article to your Knowledge Base, then open Image Studio. It reads the article and generates a matching visual — a step-by-step field guide, a labeled diagram, an infographic. The image is tailored to the content, not generic clip art.

Example: The water purification article becomes a visual field guide showing each method side-by-side: boiling setup, bleach-to-water ratios, SODIS bottle placement — all labeled and color-coded for quick reference in the field.
3
Extract a Parts List

AI Reads the Image Back to You

Send the generated image back into the Command Center chat. The AI reads every label, annotation, and detail in the image and produces a structured parts list or resource list — everything you need to actually execute what's in the diagram.

Example: From the water purification diagram, the AI extracts: clear containers (2L minimum), unscented household bleach (8.25% sodium hypochlorite), eye dropper, metal pot, heat source, chlorine dioxide tablets — with quantities and sourcing notes.

Question → Article → Visual → Parts List. That's the full pipeline. One question, and you have everything you need to act.
