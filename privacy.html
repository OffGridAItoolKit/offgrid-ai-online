Privacy Policy

OffGrid AI ToolKit app and online mobile experience. Last updated July 8, 2026.
OffGrid AI ToolKit v1 is an online AI app. It does not require an account, does not use ads, and does not use in-app purchases. Prompts, photos, videos, and generated-image requests are sent to online AI services only when you choose to use those features.

Information You Choose To Send

When you use chat, image upload, camera, video, voice, or Image Studio, the content you provide may be sent to the OffGrid AI ToolKit online service and to third-party AI processors used to generate the response. This can include text prompts, uploaded images, selected videos, extracted video frames, voice transcription text, and generated-image prompts.

Device Permissions

The app asks for permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera for taking photos or recording video.
  • Photo or media access for choosing files to upload or saving generated visuals.
  • Microphone for voice input or video recording.
  • Motion sensors for the tap-to-enable compass heading.
  • Network access for online AI responses.

Accounts, Payments, Ads, And Analytics

OffGrid AI ToolKit v1 does not require user accounts, does not include in-app purchases, does not show ads, and does not include third-party advertising analytics. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Storage And Saved Guides

When you tap Save Image, Save Guide PDF, or Export, the app saves the selected output to your phone or browser download location. Saved outputs are controlled by you and remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Operational Logs

Like most online services, the hosting platform and server may process basic technical information such as request path, time, IP address, device/browser information, and error details for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting. OffGrid AI ToolKit is designed to avoid unnecessary account-level tracking in v1.

Third-Party Processing

The app uses online AI model providers and hosting infrastructure to process requests. Their systems may receive the content needed to complete your request. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, legal, financial, or confidential information unless you are comfortable sending it to online AI services.

Safety Note

AI can make mistakes. Verify important survival, medical, legal, financial, or safety information before acting on it.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@offgridaitoolkit.com.

This policy is written for the free online v1 app-store launch and should be reviewed before final store submission.
