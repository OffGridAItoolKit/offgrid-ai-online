Privacy Policy

OffGrid AI FieldGuide app and online mobile experience. Last updated September 11, 2026.
OffGrid AI FieldGuide is an online AI app. Before an AI feature sends data, the app explains what may be sent, names the service providers, and asks for consent. Data you choose to submit travels through the OffGrid AI service hosted by Render, then OpenRouter, and then Google Vertex AI or OpenAI to complete your request. The app does not require an account, use ads, or include in-app purchases.

AI Data Consent And Withdrawal

Before any online AI feature can send data, the app presents a consent screen that identifies the categories of data and the named recipients. If you do not consent, no prompt, voice transcription text, photo, video frame, or image-generation request is sent. Non-AI device features remain available. Before the first selected photo or video transfer, the app presents a separate contextual media-transfer confirmation. That media consent remains active for future user-selected media until the user withdraws it.

You may withdraw consent at any time from + menu > Privacy & AI Data > Withdraw AI Data Consent. Withdrawal blocks future AI and media transfers and clears any currently selected media from the app interface. It does not affect a request that was already completed. You can later review the disclosure and consent again.

Information You Choose To Send

Depending on the feature you deliberately use, Apple Speech Recognition may process microphone audio to produce voice transcription text. The app may send that resulting text, text you type, a selected or captured photo, extracted frames from a selected or recorded video, an AI answer you choose to turn into a visual, or an image-generation prompt to the named AI processors below. Microphone audio used for Voice Input is not sent to the OffGrid AI application server. A selected photo is resized to a maximum of 1024 by 1024 pixels before transfer. A raw video file is not transferred; the app extracts and sends up to eight JPEG frames, each no larger than 512 by 384 pixels.

OffGrid AI FieldGuide uses submitted content only to produce the answer or visual you requested. It does not use submitted content for identity verification, advertising, marketing, user profiling, sale, or AI-model training. The content is not written to the OffGrid AI application database as a saved conversation, face database, media library, or personal account history.

Photos, Video Frames, And Face Data

Face data the app may collect: OffGrid AI FieldGuide does not intentionally collect biometric face data. If a photo or extracted video frame that you choose to send contains a person, the submitted image may incidentally contain a visible image of that person's face. That visible facial imagery is the only face-related data the app may process.

No face recognition or biometric processing: The app does not detect, recognize, identify, authenticate, map, measure, track, or compare faces. It does not create face geometry, faceprints, templates, embeddings, biometric identifiers, face profiles, or a face database. It does not use facial imagery for advertising, marketing, profiling, surveillance, eligibility decisions, or AI-model training.

Use and sharing: A face that happens to be visible in user-selected media is processed only as part of the user's requested general image or video analysis. The selected photo or extracted video frames are transmitted through the OffGrid AI service hosted by Render and OpenRouter to Google Vertex AI. They are not shared with advertisers, data brokers, other users, or any other AI model provider.

Storage and retention: Face-containing photos and video frames are handled transiently to complete the current request. They are not persisted in the OffGrid AI application database or logs and are not retained as a face or media history. The raw video remains on the device; a native temporary copy used for frame extraction is deleted after extraction. AI processing is restricted to OpenRouter Zero Data Retention endpoints with provider data collection denied and fallback providers disabled. OpenRouter and the selected Google Vertex AI endpoint do not persist prompt, response, photo, or video-frame content under this routing. Limited non-content request metadata may be processed for billing, security, abuse prevention, and reliability.

Deletion: Because OffGrid AI, OpenRouter, and the selected AI endpoint do not persist the submitted face-containing content under this configuration, there is no server-side face record to delete after processing. Before sending, a user can cancel the transfer or remove the attachment. After sending, local previews and session content can be cleared by starting a new chat, closing or reloading the app, or uninstalling it. Separately saved photos, generated images, or PDFs remain on the device until the user deletes them in Photos or Files.

Named Service Providers And Data Route

  • Apple Speech Recognition may process microphone audio when the user deliberately activates Voice Input, solely to return transcription text. The resulting text is not sent into the AI route until the user chooses to submit the question.
  • Render hosts the OffGrid AI application service, which receives the request and forwards only the content needed to complete it.
  • OpenRouter routes the request. FieldGuide requests require Zero Data Retention, deny provider data collection, and disable unapproved fallback providers.
  • Google Vertex AI processes FieldGuide text, selected photos, extracted video frames, and Google-powered image-generation requests.
  • OpenAI processes text-only visual-prompt preparation used by Image Studio. Photos and video frames are not sent to OpenAI.

These providers receive only the content and technical information needed for the user-requested function. OffGrid AI requires submitted content to be handled only for that function and uses Zero Data Retention and data-collection restrictions to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy. No provider is authorized by OffGrid AI to use submitted content for advertising, user profiling, sale, or AI-model training.

Data Retention

OffGrid AI FieldGuide does not require an account and does not maintain a cloud conversation, face, or media history. Prompt text, AI responses, uploaded photos, extracted video frames, voice transcription text, generated-image prompts, and generated images are handled transiently to complete the request and are not retained in the OffGrid AI application database after the request is completed.

  • Anonymous daily usage counters contain a one-way network-address hash, dates, counts, and timestamps, but no prompt or media content. These counters are automatically deleted after 31 days.
  • Optional feedback, content reports, and support correspondence are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate, respond, resolve the request, maintain safety, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion at any time using the process below.
  • Apple, Render, OpenRouter, Google Vertex AI, OpenAI, and security systems may process limited non-content technical metadata for their billing, security, reliability, abuse-prevention, and legal-compliance periods. OffGrid AI does not control those metadata retention periods.
  • Images, PDFs, and other exports you choose to save remain on your device until you delete them.

Data Deletion

Because OffGrid AI FieldGuide does not create user accounts or retain a cloud chat, face, or media history, there is no account-history or face-record deletion step. To request deletion of identifiable optional feedback, content reports, or support correspondence, email support@offgridaitoolkit.com with the subject OffGrid AI FieldGuide Data Deletion Request. Include the approximate submission date and enough detail for us to identify the record. After reasonable verification, we will delete the identifiable matching records unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or an active dispute.

Files saved to your phone can be deleted directly with your Gallery or Files app. Uninstalling OffGrid AI FieldGuide removes app-private data, but it may not remove files you separately saved to Downloads, Gallery, or another shared folder. See the step-by-step OffGrid AI FieldGuide Data Deletion page.

Device Permissions

The app asks for permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera for taking photos or recording video.
  • Photo or media access for choosing files to upload or saving generated visuals.
  • Microphone for voice input or video recording.
  • Motion sensors for the tap-to-enable compass heading.
  • Network access for online AI responses.

Accounts, Payments, Ads, And Analytics

OffGrid AI FieldGuide does not require user accounts, does not include in-app purchases, does not show ads, and does not include third-party advertising analytics. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Storage And Saved Guides

When you tap Save Image, Save Guide PDF, or Export, the app saves the selected output to your phone or browser download location. Saved outputs are controlled by you and remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Feedback And Content Reports

If you submit feedback or report an AI response, OffGrid AI stores the category, the details you type, basic app context, and the reported AI response only when you choose to include it. Reports do not automatically include your full chat history, account information, or uploaded media. Reports are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for product support, safety review, and resolution, and you may request deletion using the process above.

Operational Logs

Like most online services, the hosting platform and server may process basic technical information such as request path, time, IP address, device/browser information, and error details for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting. OffGrid AI does not intentionally write prompt text, response text, uploaded media, or generated-image content to its operational logs.

To enforce free daily limits without creating an account, the server stores a one-way hash derived from the network address with daily request counters. The original network address is not stored in this usage table, the counters contain no prompt or media content, and these anonymous counters are deleted after 31 days.

Third-Party Processing

The named providers above process content only when you consent and deliberately use an AI feature. OpenRouter Zero Data Retention routing rejects endpoints that would persist prompt or response content. Provider data collection is denied, and fallback providers are disabled for the disclosed routing. Technical request metadata may still be processed for billing, security, reliability, and abuse prevention. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, legal, financial, confidential, or third-party information unless you have the right to share it and are comfortable sending it to these online services.

Provider information: Render Privacy Policy, OpenRouter Privacy Policy, OpenRouter Zero Data Retention, Google Cloud Privacy Notice, and OpenAI Privacy Policy.

Safety Note

AI can make mistakes. Verify important survival, medical, legal, financial, or safety information before acting on it.
