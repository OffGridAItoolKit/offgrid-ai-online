Privacy Policy

OffGrid AI Field Guide app and online mobile experience. Last updated August 7, 2026.
OffGrid AI Field Guide is an online AI app. It does not require an account, does not use ads, and does not use in-app purchases. Prompts, photos, videos, and generated-image requests are sent to online AI services only when you choose to use those features.

Information You Choose To Send

When you use chat, image upload, camera, video, voice, or Image Studio, the content you provide may be sent to the OffGrid AI Field Guide online service and to third-party AI processors used to generate the response. This can include text prompts, uploaded images, selected videos, extracted video frames, voice transcription text, and generated-image prompts.

OffGrid AI Field Guide processes that content in memory to complete the request. It is not written to the OffGrid AI application database as a saved conversation, media library, or personal account history.

Device Permissions

The app asks for permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera for taking photos or recording video.
  • Photo or media access for choosing files to upload or saving generated visuals.
  • Microphone for voice input or video recording.
  • Motion sensors for the tap-to-enable compass heading.
  • Network access for online AI responses.

Accounts, Payments, Ads, And Analytics

OffGrid AI Field Guide does not require user accounts, does not include in-app purchases, does not show ads, and does not include third-party advertising analytics. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Storage And Saved Guides

When you tap Save Image, Save Guide PDF, or Export, the app saves the selected output to your phone or browser download location. Saved outputs are controlled by you and remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Feedback And Content Reports

If you submit feedback or report an AI response, OffGrid AI stores the category, the details you type, basic app context, and the reported AI response only when you choose to include it. Reports do not automatically include your full chat history, account information, or uploaded media. Reports are retained for product support, safety review, and resolution.

Operational Logs

Like most online services, the hosting platform and server may process basic technical information such as request path, time, IP address, device/browser information, and error details for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting. OffGrid AI does not intentionally write prompt text, response text, uploaded media, or generated-image content to its operational logs.

To enforce free daily limits without creating an account, the server stores a one-way hash derived from the network address with daily request counters. The original network address is not stored in this usage table, the counters contain no prompt or media content, and these anonymous counters are deleted after 31 days.

Third-Party Processing

The app uses OpenRouter, online AI model providers, hosting infrastructure, and operational logging services to process requests and operate the service. Their systems may receive the content or technical metadata needed to complete your request. OffGrid AI Field Guide app requests enforce OpenRouter Zero Data Retention routing, which rejects AI endpoints that would retain prompt or response content. OpenRouter input/output logging and product-improvement data use are also disabled for the account. Technical request metadata may still be processed for billing, security, reliability, and abuse prevention. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, legal, financial, or confidential information unless you are comfortable sending it to online AI services.

Learn more in the OpenRouter data collection documentation.

Safety Note

AI can make mistakes. Verify important survival, medical, legal, financial, or safety information before acting on it.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@offgridaitoolkit.com.
