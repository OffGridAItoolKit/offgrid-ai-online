FIELD GUIDE: Sudden Weather Change

  1. Check exposure first. Move away from ridgelines, isolated trees, and open water.
  2. Protect core temperature. Add a dry layer before you become chilled.
  3. Reassess the route. Turn back early if visibility, footing, or timing deteriorates.
  4. Save battery. Record your position and reduce unnecessary phone use.

Verify local conditions and use professional or emergency help when the situation requires it.
