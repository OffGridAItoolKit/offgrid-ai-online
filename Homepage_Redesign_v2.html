Frequently Asked Questions
Does it really work without any internet? +
Yes, 100% offline. All AI models run locally from the USB drive. Your data never leaves your device. No cloud, no tracking, no external connections whatsoever. This is included with every tier.
What's the difference between the three tiers? +
All three tiers include the full offline AI ToolKit on a USB drive, plus free unlimited access to the Online ToolKit. The $129 ToolKit gives you everything you need for offline AI. The $249 Command Center adds direct access to GPT-5.2, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Grok individually, plus the AI Council where all 4 deliberate together. You get 150 sessions/month shared across all modes, and 10 Image Studio generations/month. The $469 Command Center Pro increases that to 400 sessions and 30 images/month for professionals and power users. No tier requires a subscription.
What is the Command Center? +
The Command Center is a premium online feature included with the $249 and $469 tiers. You get two ways to use it. First, chat directly with any of the four frontier models one-on-one: GPT-5.2, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, or Grok 4.1 Fast. Second, use the AI Council, where all four models answer your question independently, score each other in a blind peer review, elect a Chairman, and synthesize one final answer. Your 150 sessions/month work across both modes. You can try it free right now.
What happens when I hit my monthly limit? +
Graceful degradation. When you reach your monthly Command Council or Image Studio limit, those premium features pause until the next month. But your offline ToolKit and the free Online ToolKit (Gemma 3) continue working without any interruption. You never lose access to your core AI.
What is the Knowledge Base? +
The Knowledge Base lets you save entire conversations, including generated images, directly to your USB drive while you're online. Research survival guides, medical references, homesteading tips, anything you need. When the internet goes down, everything you saved is right there on the USB, ready to pick up where you left off, completely offline.
Do the AI models update automatically? +
Yes. For Command Center and Command Center Pro tiers, the online AI models update automatically to the newest, most trusted versions. When the AI world advances, your Command Center advances with it. No extra charge. No upgrade fees. Your toolkit gets better over time.
What are the system requirements? +
For offline mode: Windows PC with 16GB RAM (32GB recommended), USB 3.0 port, and a modern processor (Intel i7 2022+ or equivalent). A dedicated GPU improves performance but isn't required. For online mode: any computer with a web browser. No special hardware needed.
Can I use it on my phone or tablet? +
Yes! Once plugged into a Windows PC, the built-in web server creates a local network. Scan the QR code to access AI from any phone, tablet, or Mac on the same Wi-Fi. No internet required. The online features also work on any mobile browser.
Is there really no subscription? +
Really. No subscription. No monthly fees. No credit card on file. You buy the drive, you own the AI. The monthly Command Council and Image Studio allocations are included with your one-time purchase and reset on the 1st of each month at no additional cost.
